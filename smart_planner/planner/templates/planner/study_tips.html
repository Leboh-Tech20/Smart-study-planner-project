Study Tips

Tip #1: Break down your study sessions

Studying in small, manageable chunks is much more effective than cramming for hours. Take breaks in between to recharge.

Tip #2: Use active recall

Instead of passively reading your notes, test yourself regularly to reinforce learning. Ask yourself questions to engage your memory.

.

Tip #3: Stay organized with a planner

Keep track of deadlines, assignments, and exams with a planner to avoid last-minute stress. Visualize your schedule to stay on track.

Tip #4: Stay hydrated and rest

Hydration and sufficient rest are key to maintaining focus and memory retention during your study sessions.
